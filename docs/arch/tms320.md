# TMS320C15

Part of [Development style](../development-style.md): read General first.

**Pitfalls.** The bus timing has no slack: the 10 ns delays after each
drive and before each sample are the setup margin; don't remove them.

The profile (`tools/cycles_tms320c15.py`, recorded as
`tools/tms320c15-cycles.jsonl.zst`) matches the tables on every opcode.
**[hw]** The debugger drives `#BIO`, so `BIOZ` is recorded both taken and
not.

The fetch data settles as late as the CLKIN edge after it. **[hw]** The
10 ns delays settle it 40 ns before that edge and 110 ns before `#MEN`
rises, and hold it 120 ns after, with analyser leads on the bus too. Too
little margin shows as a few wrong mandelbrot pixels at the same places,
not every frame.
