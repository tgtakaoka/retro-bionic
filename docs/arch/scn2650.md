# SCN2650

Part of [Development style](../development-style.md): read General first.

**Pitfalls.** An indirect `BCTR`, `BDRA` and the like skip their indirect
address read when they fall through, two bus cycles fewer than their row
**[hw]**; `HALT` makes no bus cycle after its fetch.

The profile is `tools/cycles_scn2650.py`, recorded as
`tools/scn2650-cycles.jsonl.zst`. `InstScn2650::notTaken()` tells a
fall-through by the address after the operand, so the run loop and the
matcher don't take those cycles from the next instruction.
